BEHIND THE ESTIMATE

A clear path from inputs to insight.

  1. Share your profile

    Nine fields match the saved model’s exact inputs. Currency and income units must match the training dataset.

  2. Let the pipeline work

    The saved pipeline handles imputation, scaling and encoding before the Random Forest estimates the historical approval label.

  3. Understand your outlook

    A probability of 50% or above maps to “Loan Approval Likely.” Probabilities are model estimates and have not been independently calibrated.

This local tool does not contact credit bureaus or lenders. It is intended for exploration, not real lending decisions.
